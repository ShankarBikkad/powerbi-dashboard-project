# Power BI Data Professional Survey Dashboard

A beginner-friendly **Power BI** project where I turned survey data from data professionals into an interactive dashboard.

## Dataset
- **File:** `Power BI - Final Project.xlsx` (about 630 survey responses)
- **Includes:** job title, salary range, industry, country, age, gender, favorite programming language and happiness ratings (salary, work/life balance and more)

## What I Did
1. **Loaded the data** into Power BI and prepared it for reporting.
2. **Cleaned and shaped the columns** so they are easy to use in charts.
3. **Built a one-page dashboard** called "Data Professional Survey Breakdown" with:
   - **Cards** – total survey takers and average age
   - **Treemap** – where survey takers live (by country)
   - **Bar chart** – job titles
   - **Column chart** – favorite programming language by job title
   - **Donut chart** – average salary by gender
   - **Gauges** – happiness with salary and with work/life balance
4. **Added filters** so the dashboard can be explored by job title and salary.

## Quick Facts From the Data
- Data Analyst is the most common job title in the survey.
- Most survey takers live in the United States, followed by India and the United Kingdom.
